About EcoSafe Bangladesh

Environmental protection. Social development. Shared responsibility.

01 / Our purpose

EcoSafe Bangladesh is a non-profit society focused on environmental protection, sustainability, social development, research and community empowerment.

Its work brings together awareness, consultancy, research dissemination and practical initiatives that respond to environmental and social challenges. The organization aims to help people build the knowledge and skills needed for healthier lives and sustainable livelihoods.

02 / Mission & vision
Mission

To empower people with knowledge, skills, and awareness to address environmental and social challenges, improve hygiene and health, and build sustainable livelihoods.

Vision

To build a greener, healthier, and more inclusive Bangladesh through environmental protection, education, and community empowerment.
